Teach Headway your voice.

The audio-only Headway: a person speaks, a speech model tuned to them writes it down, the AI4A11y Controller works out what they mean, and Chrome does it on the page. Onboarding is where the tuning happens, and where the person sees, in their own recordings, why it matters. Sentences, scores and transcripts on this board are sample values, not results.

How one command travels

Faridah speaks → the browser acts

Chrome control portOne ControlPort over chrome.tabs, scripting and debugger. Pages Chrome protects (chrome://, the Web Store) say so instead of failing quietly.
Default to askingUnclear speech never runs. She gets the transcript and two big guesses to choose from, and a choice can be remembered.
